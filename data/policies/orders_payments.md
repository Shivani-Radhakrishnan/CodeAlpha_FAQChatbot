# Orders and payments

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an order within 12 hours of placing it, from Orders in your account, as long as it has not been dispatched. After 12 hours, or once it has shipped, you can no longer cancel it, but you can return it when it arrives.

## Changing an order
You can change the items or quantities in an order within 1 hour of placing it by contacting support. After that, the order goes to our warehouse and cannot be changed; you can cancel it within 12 hours instead and place a new one.

## Accepted payment methods
We accept credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal and net banking. We do not offer cash on delivery; all orders are prepaid. You can pay part of an order with a gift card and the rest with another method.

## Paying in installments
Orders over $100 can be split into 4 interest-free payments at checkout. The first payment is taken when you order and the rest every two weeks. A missed installment is retried after 3 days; there is no late fee.

## Payment security
All payments are encrypted and processed by a PCI-compliant payment provider. We never store full card numbers; only the last four digits are kept to help you recognise the card.

## Why an order is cancelled by us
We may cancel an order if the payment fails, if the item turns out to be out of stock, or if our fraud checks flag the order. You are always told by email, and any payment taken is refunded in full within 5-7 business days.

## Promo codes
You can apply one promo code per order, on the payment screen at checkout. Promo codes cannot be combined with each other or with the student discount, and they do not apply to gift cards.

# Rewards, discounts and gift cards

## BotBuddy Rewards
Joining BotBuddy Rewards is free. You earn 1 point for every $1 you spend, and every 100 points can be redeemed for $5 off a future order. Points are added once the return window for an order has closed. Points expire if your account has no purchases for 12 months.

## Rewards tiers
Members who spend more than $500 in a calendar year become Gold members for the following year. Gold members earn 2 points per $1, get free express shipping on orders over $100, and get free returns on every order.

## Student discount
Students get 10% off with a valid student ID, verified once a year through our student verification page. The student discount cannot be combined with promo codes and does not apply to gift cards or BotBuddy Care.

## Price matching
If you find the same item cheaper at another major retailer within 14 days of your purchase, we refund the difference. Submit a request with a link to the competitor's offer. Price matching does not apply to marketplace sellers, auctions, clearance sales or flash sales lasting less than 24 hours.

## Gift cards
Digital gift cards from $10 to $500 are sold on our website and never expire. They are delivered by email, can be used over several orders, and cannot be exchanged for cash. If a gift card is lost, we can replace it when you send us the purchase confirmation.

## Current offers
Seasonal sales are announced by email and on the homepage. Sale prices are valid only while the sale runs, and items bought at a sale price are refunded at the price you paid.

# Your account and privacy

## Creating an account
Click "Sign Up" on our website or in the app and enter your email address and a password. You need an account to track orders, start returns and earn rewards points, but you can also check out as a guest.

## Passwords and sign-in
If you forget your password, click "Forgot Password" on the login screen. We email you a reset link that expires after 1 hour. You can turn on two-step verification under Account Settings > Security, which asks for a code from an authenticator app when you sign in on a new device.

## Deleting your account
Go to Account Settings > Privacy and choose "Delete account". Your personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days. We keep order and invoice records for 7 years because tax law requires it, but they are no longer linked to a profile you can sign in to.

## How we use your data
We use your data to process orders, provide support and, if you agree, send marketing emails. We do not sell your personal data to anyone. We share it only with the services needed to deliver your order, such as payment providers and carriers.

## Emails and cookies
You can unsubscribe from marketing emails with the link at the bottom of any email or in your account settings; order and delivery emails are always sent. Our website uses essential cookies to work and asks before setting analytics or advertising cookies, which you can switch off at any time under Cookie Settings.

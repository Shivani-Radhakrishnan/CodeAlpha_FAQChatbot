# Shipping and delivery

## Delivery times
Standard shipping within the United States usually takes 5-7 business days after your order is dispatched. Express delivery takes 1-2 business days. Orders placed before 2 pm Eastern Time on a business day are dispatched the same day; later orders are dispatched the next business day.

## Shipping fees
Standard shipping is free on orders over $50. Smaller orders pay a flat fee of $4.99. Express delivery costs $14.99 per order, whatever the order value.

## International shipping
We ship to most countries worldwide. International delivery usually takes 7-14 business days. Customs duties and import taxes are not included in the price and are paid by the customer on delivery. We currently cannot ship to P.O. boxes outside the United States, or to countries under international trade sanctions.

## Tracking your order
When your order ships, we email you a tracking link. You can also see the tracking number and live status under Orders in your account. Tracking information can take up to 24 hours to appear after dispatch.

## Late deliveries
If your order is late, we will update you by email. If an express order arrives after its promised delivery date, we refund the express shipping fee automatically. For standard orders delayed by more than 10 business days, contact support and we may offer a discount on your next order as compensation.

## Changing the delivery address
You can change the delivery address from your account settings until the order is shipped. After dispatch, the address can no longer be changed, but you can ask the carrier to redirect the parcel using the tracking link.

## Packaging
We ship in recyclable cardboard and paper packaging and avoid single-use plastic wherever we can. Gift wrapping can be chosen at checkout for $3.99 per item and includes a handwritten note.

# Customer support and company information

## Contacting support
Our support team is available 24/7 by live chat on the website and in the app, and by email at support@example.com. Email replies usually arrive within 12 hours. You can ask the chat assistant to connect you to a human agent at any time by typing "human".

## Phone support
We do not offer phone support. Live chat is the fastest way to reach us, and chat agents can arrange a call back for warranty claims that are easier to explain by phone.

## About the company
We are an online-only store headquartered in New York, USA. We do not have physical stores, which helps us keep prices low. Our app is available for iOS and Android.

## Accessibility
Our website and app support screen readers, keyboard navigation and adjustable text size. If you need help placing an order because of a disability, our chat agents can place it with you, and you can ask for order emails in large print.

# Warranty

## What the warranty covers
Every product comes with a 1-year warranty from the date of delivery. It covers defects in materials and workmanship that appear with normal use, such as a device that stops charging or a zip that breaks.

## What the warranty does not cover
The warranty does not cover accidental damage (drops, spills, cracked screens), normal wear and tear, batteries losing capacity over time, damage from unauthorised repairs, or cosmetic marks that do not affect how the product works.

## Making a warranty claim
To make a claim, open the order in your account and choose "Report a problem", then describe the fault and add a photo or video. We reply within 2 business days. If the claim is approved, we repair or replace the product at no cost, including shipping; if neither is possible, we refund the price you paid.

## Extended protection
For electronics, you can add BotBuddy Care at checkout, which extends the warranty by 2 more years (3 years in total) and adds cover for one accidental-damage repair. BotBuddy Care costs 10% of the product price and must be bought with the product.

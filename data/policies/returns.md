# Returns, exchanges and refunds

## Return window
You can return an item within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused, in its original condition and packaging, and you need your receipt or order confirmation email. Start a return from the Returns Center in your account; you will get a return label and a drop-off code.

## Items that cannot be returned
Some items cannot be returned unless they arrive faulty: gift cards, personalised or engraved products, opened earbuds and in-ear headphones, cosmetics whose seal has been broken, and anything bought as "final sale". Final-sale items are marked on the product page and in your order confirmation.

## Return shipping costs
Returns of faulty, damaged or wrongly sent items are free. For any other return, a return shipping fee of $5.95 is taken from your refund. BotBuddy Rewards Gold members get free returns on every order.

## Refund timing
We inspect returned items within 2 business days of receiving them. Once approved, the refund is sent to your original payment method within 5-7 business days. Refunds for orders paid with a gift card go back to the gift card. Original shipping fees are refunded only when the return is because of our mistake.

## Exchanges
You can exchange an item for a different size or colour of the same product within 30 days from the Returns Center. Exchanges are free and the new item ships as soon as we scan your return. If the new size or colour costs more, you pay the difference; if it costs less, we refund the difference.

## Damaged, faulty or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged, send us a photo of the item and its packaging within 7 days of delivery. We will ship a replacement or give you a full refund, whichever you prefer, and you do not need to send the damaged item back unless we ask. If you received the wrong item, contact support with your order number and we will send the correct item along with a free return label.
